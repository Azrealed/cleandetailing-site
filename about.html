A little about us

From a temporary summer gig to professional mobile detailing across Redding and Shasta County.

We started Clean Detailing in May of 2019 as just a temporary summer gig. Little did we know it would become so much more than we could have dreamed. We are constantly blown away by the level of support and love we’ve received over the last five years.

We started out with just a beat-up vacuum, but we’ve grown to offer professional mobile detailing to Redding CA and Shasta County. Our vision is not only to make your car look and feel amazing, but to give you the time to do the things you love.

There’s nothing like the peace of mind of having a clean car. We are so thankful for the amazing feedback and support we have received from our clients — we couldn’t have done it without you!
